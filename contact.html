Consultation and resource requests

Every parcel has a history. We start by listening to it.

Most of our work begins with a phone call or a note like the one below: a landowner wondering what's possible on forty acres, a town wanting a wetland assessed, a family sitting on a woodlot they inherited and aren't sure what to do with.

Send us a few details about the property, what you're hoping to accomplish, and any deadlines you're working against. We'll get back to you within two business days with next steps, or a referral if we're not the right fit for the job.

For resource requests, permit checklists, species lists, cost-share program guides, mention that in your message and we'll point you to what we have on file.
