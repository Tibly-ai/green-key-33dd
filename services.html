What We Offer

Services for land that has to outlast us.

Green Key Resources works alongside landowners, municipalities, and timber operators who want their ground managed with a plan, not a guess. Consulting, certification, and the resources to keep it all standing up to scrutiny.

01

Forest & Land Consulting

We walk your acreage before we write a word. Stand assessments, harvest planning, habitat improvement, invasive species management. Most of our clients own between 20 and 500 acres of mixed New England forest, and every plan we hand back is built around what that specific ground can support, not a template.

  • Stewardship and management plans
  • Timber harvest planning and marking
  • Wildlife habitat and trail design
02

Certification & Compliance

Getting a parcel certified, or keeping it that way, takes paperwork most landowners have no interest in doing alone. We prepare and manage FSC and SFI documentation, run pre-audit walkthroughs, and handle chain-of-custody records so a certification renewal doesn't turn into a scramble.

  • FSC and SFI certification prep
  • Audit readiness and mock inspections
  • Chain-of-custody recordkeeping
03

Sustainability Resource Planning

For clients thinking beyond the next harvest cycle, we research and structure the programs that pay land to stay land: carbon credit enrollment, conservation easements, and state and federal grant applications for watershed and riparian protection work.

  • Carbon credit program enrollment
  • Conservation easement structuring
  • Grant research for watershed protection
