Our Approach

Forestry is patient work. We've stayed patient for three generations.

We started as three foresters marking timber sales by hand in the hills north of Ashford. The work has changed, GPS units replaced compasses, drone imagery flags what used to take a week on foot, but the questions we ask a landowner haven't. What do you want this land to do for your family, and how do we keep it doing that for the next fifty years?

Today Green Key Resources writes management plans, holds conservation easements, and supervises harvests for private landowners, land trusts, and small municipalities. Every plan gets walked before it gets written.

Management Plans

A plan you can actually read

We write forest management plans in plain language, not forester jargon, so you understand exactly what's being cut, when, and why. Plans cover stand inventory, wildlife habitat, invasive species, and a ten-year harvest schedule, and most qualify landowners for current use tax programs.

Conservation Easements

Land that stays land

We work with landowners and land trusts to draft and hold easements that keep working forest intact, and then we do the annual monitoring visits that keep those easements enforceable. No boilerplate documents, every easement is negotiated around what the family actually wants to protect.

Harvest Oversight

We stand in the woods, not the office

When a harvest is underway, one of us is on site checking mark compliance, water bar placement, and stream buffers, not just reviewing photos afterward. Landowners get a walkthrough at close of season and a straight answer about what the timber actually brought.
